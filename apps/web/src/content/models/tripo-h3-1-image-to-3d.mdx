## A single image into a 3D model

Tripo H3.1 Image to 3D uses the pinned `v3.1-20260211` model and accepts exactly one JPEG, PNG, or WebP reference up to 20 MB. The reference replaces the text prompt for this variant. Remora immediately copies the completed GLB and, when available, its rendered preview into durable storage.

## Output controls

Standard geometry accepts a face limit up to 1,500,000 and detailed geometry accepts up to 2,000,000. An empty face limit remains adaptive. Texture can be off, standard, or detailed; standard and detailed texture modes request PBR output.

The stored model is available in Remora’s orbit viewer and through an authenticated GLB download. If the rendered preview cannot be stored, the GLB is still usable and Remora shows a 3D fallback tile.

## Pricing

Tripo lists image-to-3D H3.1 generation at 20 credits without texture, 30 credits with standard texture, and 40 credits with detailed texture. Detailed geometry adds 20 credits. One Tripo credit is documented as $0.01.
