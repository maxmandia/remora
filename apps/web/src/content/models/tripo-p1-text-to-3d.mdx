## Prompt-driven low-poly generation

Tripo P1 Text to 3D uses the pinned `P1-20260311` model. It accepts prompts up to 1,024 characters and is intended for compact, low-poly 3D assets. Remora imports the resulting GLB before the provider URL expires and exposes it through an interactive viewer and durable download.

## Face and texture controls

An explicit P1 face limit can range from 50 through 20,000. Leaving the field empty uses adaptive sizing. Texture can be disabled, standard, or detailed; standard and detailed settings request PBR materials. P1 does not expose H3.1’s separate geometry-quality control.

## Pricing

Tripo documents P1 text-to-3D generation at 30 credits without texture, 40 credits with standard texture, and 50 credits with detailed texture. Tripo defines a credit as $0.01.
